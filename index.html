The headline is that fifteen inner-city catchments have 51.9% of households without a motor vehicle, and eight of those have no convenience-format store within a walkable kilometre while a competitor sits closer. The more useful finding came from reading the co-operative's published accounts: at a 3.17% margin the recapture opportunity is worth about $0.46m, while allocating $254m of annual capital 5% better is worth around $12.7m. The catchment gaps are not the prize — they are the evidence the method finds things.
